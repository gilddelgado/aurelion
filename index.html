PHASE 01 // CHASSIS

MONOCOQUE

The passenger cell, roof included, is a single carbon-fibre shell that weighs 147.5 kg. Aluminium frames bolted to it front and rear carry the suspension, and the whole structure resists 35,000 Nm of twisting force per degree.

35,000
NM/DEG TORSIONAL STIFFNESS
147.5

PHASE 02 // POWERTRAIN

PURE V12

A new 6.5-litre V12, mounted low behind the cabin, makes 700 CV at 8,250 rpm with no turbos and no electric help. The 7-speed ISR gearbox sits ahead of it in the centre tunnel and shifts in 50 milliseconds, and a Haldex IV coupling varies how much drive goes to the front wheels.

700
CV AT 8,250 RPM
50

PHASE 03 // AERODYNAMICS

SHAPING AIR

The rear wing lies flush with the engine cover until the car needs it, then rises to one of two angles: 4° for stability at high speed, or 11° for more downforce at mid-range speeds. The side air intakes open and close on their own to match how much cooling the engine needs.

WING AT 11°: MORE DOWNFORCE AT MID-RANGE SPEEDS
